# Hip rehabilitation basics

Recovery plans are individual and guided by your assessment, goals, and context. The notes below are general and do not replace advice from a registered healthcare professional.

## What influences progress?

- Day-to-day activity levels and routines
- Sleep, stress, and overall health
- Previous experiences with exercise or rehabilitation

## Common elements in a plan

- Gradual exposure to movements that matter to you
- Strength and control work matched to your current capacity
- Simple ways to monitor symptoms and adjust pacing

If you have questions about your situation, a structured assessment can help set priorities and next steps.
