# Common myths about shoulder pain

Information online can be confusing. These brief points offer general perspective and should not be taken as diagnosis or treatment.

## Myth 1: “Pain always means damage”

Pain is influenced by many factors, including sensitivity and context. An assessment can help understand what matters in your case.

## Myth 2: “Rest is the only answer”

Short periods of relative rest can be helpful, but a plan often includes graded activity and strength work.

## Myth 3: “Clicks or sounds are always harmful”

Joint sounds can occur in people with and without symptoms. Your clinician can advise if any specific tests or steps are needed.

For personalised guidance, consider booking an assessment with a registered professional.
